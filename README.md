# Quantitative Finance Portfolio

Personal and academic quantitative finance projects developed during my Master’s studies with a double degree between **Politecnico di Milano** and **École Centrale de Nantes**. 


---

    ## Projects Overview

    ### Statistical Arbitrage on EURO STOXX 50 (Avellaneda & Lee, 2008)
    An equity market-neutral quantitative strategy on EURO STOXX 50 constituents. It uses PCA factor decomposition to isolate systematic risk and models cumulative return residuals via an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (O-U) process to trade mean-reverting $s$-score signals.

    ---

    ### S&P 500 Volatility Forecasting & Variance Risk Premium (VRP)
    Econometric study comparing option-implied surface dynamics (ATM, skew, convexity, PCA) against historical benchmarks (HAR-RV, EGARCH) for predicting 21-day forward realized volatility. Features expanding-window OOS evaluation, regime-shift sensitivity analysis (Volmageddon), and a Variance Swap trading strategy backtest.
